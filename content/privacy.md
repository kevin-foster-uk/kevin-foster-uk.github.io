## Who is responsible

This site is operated by Kevin Foster (kevin.foster.uk@gmail.com). If you have any questions about how your data is handled, or wish to exercise your rights, contact that address.

## What data is collected and why

This site uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand how visitors use it — which pages are read, how long sessions last, and where visitors come from (country/region). This helps improve the content.

Google Analytics collects:

- Pages visited and time spent
- Approximate location (country and region — not city or street level)
- Device type, operating system, and browser
- Referring website

It does **not** collect your name, email address, or any information that directly identifies you. No data is collected until you explicitly accept below.

## Legal basis

Data is processed only with your consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) / EU GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You may withdraw consent at any time by clicking **Decline** in the cookie banner. To see the banner again, open your browser's developer tools, go to **Application → Local Storage**, and delete the `ga-consent` entry for this site, then reload the page.

## Data transfers

Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC, which is based in the United States. The transfer is covered by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses, providing appropriate safeguards under UK/EU data protection law.

## Data retention

Analytics data is retained in Google Analytics for **2 months**, after which it is automatically deleted.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the data held about you
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten")
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (see above)
- **Lodge a complaint** with the ICO (UK): [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk), or your national supervisory authority (EU)

To exercise any of these rights, contact kevin.foster.uk@gmail.com.

## No other tracking

No other analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used on this site.
